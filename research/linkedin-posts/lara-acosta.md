**Topic:** A practical goal-setting framework for the new year — converting vague "dreams" into specific, measurable, monthly-broken-down goals, illustrated with her own real example

**Full post text:**
The best career advice I can give you this 2026 (read this before you plan your year): Stop setting dreams. Start setting goals.

Instead of writing down in a journal "Hit 100k in profit," follow my proven goal-setting framework that works. Write down the goal: "Hit 100k in profit in 2026." Write down the overview: "Launch 3 new offers." This helps make it simple and achievable.

Now, we need to add urgency and measurability: split the goal into tasks required to do every month. January: finish the landing page, launch a waitlist. February: post 4x pieces of promo content on Fridays. March: launch new offer, schedule webinars and deliver.

This is how I've planned every launch I did this year. I turned my "dreams" into small and actionable "goals." Don't spend another year dreaming. DO it instead.

## Post 3

**Topic:** Her named "SLAY Framework" for LinkedIn growth through storytelling — a specific 4-step post structure she credits for growing to 300k+ followers without burning out

**Full post text:**
There's 100s of ways to get more followers on LinkedIn. But this one got me to 300k+ (without burning out). I called it the SLAY Framework:

1. The first 3 lines of your post should be a story.
2. Then on line 4, lead with a lesson for your audience.
3. Add to the post using actionable advice (listicles).
4. Finish with a question that puts the ball in their court.

Storytelling is the ultimate weapon for LinkedIn growth. And the easiest way to build trust that converts clients.

3 things to remember: make sure you use "you" at least twice in your post; use a listicle or bullet points to give context faster; use the story to educate or inspire your audience.

It's time you tell your story.
