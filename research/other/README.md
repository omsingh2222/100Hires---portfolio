Other Materials

This folder is for any supporting research that doesn't fit into LinkedIn posts or YouTube transcripts. Examples:





Podcast transcript excerpts where an expert discusses LinkedIn/content strategy



Screenshots of high-performing posts (if text copy isn't fully capturable)



Articles or newsletter issues written by the experts



Notes from any talks or webinars by the experts

Format

For each item, save as a markdown file with:

# [Title of material]
**Source:** [Expert name]
**Type:** [Podcast / Article / Newsletter / Talk]
**Link:** [URL]
**Date:** [date]

## Summary / Key excerpt
[your notes or the relevant excerpt]

## Relevance
[why this supports the LinkedIn content strategy research]
