## Key framework: the Win Map™
Talya's consultancy is built around a named, trademarked deliverable called the Win Map™ — described as "the narrative arc that drives your deck, one pagers, and any other asset your team produces - from outreach to website copy." This is the productized version of the "sales deck audit" critique style already visible in her LinkedIn posts.

**Core philosophy, in her own words:**
"I don't need seven workshops. Three honest conversations with your SMEs and a look at what you're currently doing tells me where the narrative breaks and why your team doesn't agree on the story." This mirrors her LinkedIn post style — direct, low-tolerance-for-fluff diagnostic language.

**On the real competitive threat in B2B sales:**
"Your biggest competitive threat isn't who you think. It's your buyer deciding to do nothing." This reframes competitive positioning away from rival vendors and toward status-quo bias — a notably different angle than typical "battlecard vs. competitor X" content.

**On win-loss analysis methodology (from the Clozd interview):**
She emphasized varying analysis approaches per company rather than running the same generic process everywhere: "Don't just use the same format, the same type of analysis... It is not the same deck."

**On how her framework differs from existing positioning methodologies:**
She positions her work as complementary to, not competing with, established frameworks: "April Dunford's framework is excellent for defining your competitive positioning — what you are and why you win... I build the narrative layer that turns positioning into assets that actually get used in sales conversations." This shows clear awareness of where she sits in the broader competitive-positioning landscape relative to other named experts in the field.
