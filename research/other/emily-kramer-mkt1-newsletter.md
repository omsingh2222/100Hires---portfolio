## About MKT1

MKT1 is Emily Kramer's newsletter focused on B2B startup marketing strategy, marketing org design, campaign systems, and channels/tactics for the AI era, written roughly twice a month for an audience of around 80,000 marketers. Emily is the former first Head of Marketing at Asana and VP Marketing at Carta, and writes what she calls "Krameworks" — original frameworks for building B2B marketing functions.

## Summary / Key excerpt

This specific newsletter issue is part of a 3-part series where Emily analyzed 100 B2B marketing teams, compiling 19 charts and graphs across 90 data points per company, then wrote up the findings as a "State of B2B Marketing" report. Notably, the report itself was built using Claude Code — she explicitly documents the process of using Claude Code to analyze and visualize the dataset, and made the underlying data accessible as a Skill in her MKT1 MCP Server for paid subscribers.

Related newsletter content from the same general period (her "Gen Marketer Summit" recap) reinforces a consistent theme in her recent work: the idea of a "Gen Marketer" — meaning marketers in the AI era need to become generalists who can connect skills across growth, product, content, and partner marketing rather than staying narrowly specialized, since AI has made it easier to build products, create content, and access data, raising the bar for what counts as baseline marketing competency.

## Relevance

This is directly relevant to the research topic (LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS) in two ways. First, it reinforces a pattern already visible in Emily's LinkedIn posts collected separately in this research (`research/linkedin-posts/emily-kramer.md`): she consistently uses her own real, in-progress work (building a Claude skill, analyzing data with Claude Code) as the actual content itself, rather than writing about marketing in the abstract. Second, it's a strong example of repurposing a single piece of deep research (the 100-company dataset) across multiple formats and channels — a 3-part newsletter series, a live summit keynote, video clips, and a downloadable Skill for her MCP server — which is a useful model for how a long-form research project like the one being conducted for this 100Hires assignment could eventually be repurposed into shorter, distributable content pieces.

It also surfaces a broader industry concept (the "Gen Marketer" — AI-era marketing generalist) that's a useful piece of context for understanding why companies and creators across this entire research set (Dharmesh Shah's "AX," Natalie Marcotullio's MCP post, Tas Bober's Codex usage) are all independently converging on the same theme: AI tooling fluency is becoming a core, expected marketing skill rather than a specialized one, as of mid-2026.
