# Lashay Lewis — BOFU.ai Playbook & Founder Story

**Source:** Lashay Lewis

**Date:** Late 2025 – early 2026

## Summary / Key excerpt

Lashay Lewis is the founder of BOFU.ai, a human-led, AI-powered consultancy specializing in bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) content for B2B SaaS companies, with past clients including Conveyor, Teal, and SoundThinking. Her business evolved from an earlier consultancy, Authority Plug (which is how she's described in `sources.md`), into BOFU.ai once she began layering AI tooling into her productized content service.

Her origin story is notable: according to a ClearVoice interview, her pivot into bottom-of-funnel content specifically came after being rejected from a role she wanted because she "didn't know how to write that kind of content well yet" — a rejection she describes as having sparked the deep specialization that eventually became her business.

A consistent theme across multiple sources is her philosophy that AI should function as a "co-pilot working within a proven human framework" rather than a replacement for strategic thinking — she explicitly notes that many AI writing tools struggle with BOFU content not because the technology is flawed, but because the necessary business context and inputs are often missing or fragmented across teams (product, sales, marketing). Her solution was spending roughly two years refining her own content frameworks before "teaching those processes to AI," rather than relying on off-the-shelf AI writing tools.

Her playbook (available as a free download on bofu.ai) is built around the idea that most content strategies were built for "a version of Google that doesn't exist anymore," and that she reverse-engineered how AI models like ChatGPT and Gemini select sources to cite, after analyzing hundreds of search results. Several quotes from a related webinar recap reinforce her core philosophy: "AI is less about keywords, and more about persona," and "People don't have a bottom of funnel problem, they have an internal communication issue."

## Relevance

This material substantially deepens the context already collected on Lashay Lewis from her LinkedIn posts (`research/linkedin-posts/lashay-lewis.md`), which referenced her "big playbook" for building BOFU content systems with Claude but didn't go into detail on the underlying philosophy. It directly reinforces a pattern visible across multiple experts in this entire research project: successful B2B SaaS practitioners increasingly position AI as an accelerant layered on top of a proven human methodology, rather than a replacement for strategic judgment — the same framing used by Dharmesh Shah ("UX + AX," humans and agents working together) and Shiv Narayanan ("AI is not a silver bullet for a broken GTM engine"). It's also a useful real-world case study of a solo/small consultancy productizing and partially automating a service offering using custom AI tooling, which is directly relevant to understanding how content strategy and AI tooling intersect for the B2B SaaS practitioners being studied in this research.
