## Key quotes and takeaways

**On why interactive demos beat traditional video walkthroughs:**
"A video is generally a 30-minute walkthrough where you have to go through the entire thing to find that one section you care about. With interactive demos, you can choose your own adventure."

**On demo length — less is more:**
"Our best performing interactive demos are only about five to 10 steps. So, it's really simple and straightforward."

**On embedding conversion points without forcing the funnel:**
"Throughout interactive demos, embed CTAs. Every five to 10 steps, give them the option to keep going or see if they want to talk to your team or sign up for a free trial."

**On what interactive demos are actually for (and not for):**
"Interactive demos are meant to get users or visitors to your website to go watch the full-length demo and to go do your free trial. It's not meant to replace those things necessarily."

**On her personal motivation for joining Navattic — buyer empathy:**
She described being a frustrated B2B software buyer herself before joining, having to "wait a month" before ever seeing a product in action, which shaped her belief that buyers deserve to self-serve their way to understanding a product earlier in the funnel.

**On the real cost of going PLG:**
She pushed back on the idea that product-led growth is a simple switch to flip — "I don't think a lot of companies recognize it's a lot of engineering's resources and entirely re-shifting your go-to-market strategy."

**On Navattic's own highest-ROI channel:**
Word of mouth — driven specifically by prospects encountering a Navattic-powered interactive demo embedded on someone else's website, then clicking through the "Powered by Navattic" watermark.
