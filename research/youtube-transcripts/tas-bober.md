**On the FAQ block being the most important part of a landing page — her counter-intuitive finding:**

> **[202s]** "if you ask someone like what's the most important part of your landing page, someone would say well obviously the hero... but the truth is after dozens and dozens of experiments the actual most important part is the FAQ block which is so much further down on the page."

**On the Chipotle framework — her modular landing page writing method:**

> **[1504s]** "I call it the Chipotle framework... > **[1568s]** "the core set of information at a company captured in that buyer's journey map and those activities is essentially the same... those are all the ingredients. So I actually write my landing pages in a modular fashion."

## Key takeaways for this research
